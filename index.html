First in, first out

Little critters called pips wait in their burrows. Tap one to send it out. If its home is ready, it walks straight there. If not, it waits in the Flow Line, and the line moves strictly first in, first out.

Homes take one color at a time and the line is short, so the order you choose is the whole puzzle. Plan it, keep the line moving and fill every home.

100 gardens, ten worlds

From the Meadow to the Starlit Summit. New ideas arrive gently: switches, Nooks where a pip can rest, color gates, and a Stretch that lends one more slot.

A fresh puzzle every day

The Daily Flow is a new garden each day, the same for every player, with its own Game Center leaderboard.

At your own pace

No level timers. Undo, hints and rewinding to the last solvable moment are always free.

Fair lives

A life is used only when you give up a jammed attempt at a new level. Replays, Challenge runs and the Daily Flow never cost one.

Things to collect

Up to three stars per garden, Pixel Postcards revealed piece by piece, a daily gift, daily missions and new garden looks.

Yours on every device

Optional Sign in with Apple keeps your garden in your own iCloud. Game Center is there if you want it. Playing as a guest is complete.

Free to play

Pixel Home is free to play. Optional in-app purchases offer extra lives, 24 hours of unlimited lives and Stretch boosters; the daily gift, missions and awards give the same things for free. From version 1.1 the game is supported by ads that never interrupt a level: a short one after every fifth garden you clear, and a video you can choose to watch for an extra life.

We collect no data, and the ads are never personalized. Read the privacy policy, or visit support if you need a hand.

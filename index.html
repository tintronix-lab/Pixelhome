First in, first out

Little critters called pips wait in their burrows. Tap one to send it out. If its home is ready, it walks straight there. If not, it waits in the Flow Line, and the line moves strictly first in, first out.

Homes take one color at a time and the line is short, so the order you choose is the whole puzzle. Plan it, keep the line moving and fill every home.

Meet the pips

Six little pips live in the gardens, each with a color, a symbol and a habit of their own. Every pip and home carries its symbol, so you never have to tell colors apart by hue alone.

Posy

The welcomer · rose, with a heart

Posy grew up in the rose hedge at the edge of the Meadow and hasn’t missed a homecoming since. The little heart on top beats faster every time a pip reaches its own front door, and after a good Trio it glows for the rest of the day.

Dew

The patient one · blue, with a drop

Dew arrived at Brookside with the first rain and liked the sound of the water too much to leave. While the others fidget in the Flow Line, Dew hums along with the brook, happy to wait a turn. The droplet on top catches the morning light.

Sprig

The explorer · green, with a leaf

Sprig sprouted from a seed someone dropped on a garden path and has been poking into every corner ever since. The two leaves on top twitch whenever something new is near: a switch to turn, a Nook to peek into, a path nobody has walked yet.

Sunny

The bright spark · golden, with a star

Sunny found a crown of sunbeams on the Starlit Summit and hasn’t taken it off once. Cheerful, loud and just a little bit of a show-off, Sunny loves a long Flow more than anything, and the crown glints whenever one gets going.

Luna

The night owl · plum, with a moon

Luna sleeps through the busy part of the day, curled up in a Nook, and wakes when the moon comes up. The glowing orb on Luna’s antenna lights the paths of the Moonlit gardens, so no pip ever has to find home in the dark.

Cinder

The brave one · amber, with a flower

Cinder came down from Ember Ridge, warm as a hearth and twice as quick. Those pointed ears can hear a switch turn from across the garden. Tucked away somewhere, Cinder keeps a pressed flower from the Meadow, to remember the very first home.

A story for every pip

Each pip also has an adventure of its own: eight gardens and a finale, its story told in little pixel panels. Every friend you make leaves a keepsake in your garden.

A home of your own

Every star you earn is yours to spend in My Garden, the plot behind the title screen: houses, trees and lanterns from every world, paths, ponds and flower beds. The pips you send home live there among what you place and wear the scarves and bow ties you earn. Awards bring gifts like a fountain or a golden pip, and you can share a picture of it all. Garden visits: share a link, and a friend can look around your garden.

320 gardens, twenty-two worlds

From the Meadow to the Bamboo Grove, new ideas arrive gently: switches that redirect the path, Nooks where a pip can rest, color gates, a Stretch that lends you one more slot, and Skippers who cut in line. Clear one of the first ten worlds and it opens again by moonlight, with harder gardens on paths you know. New gardens arrive every week or two, without waiting for an update.

A fresh puzzle every day

The Daily Flow is a new garden each day, the same for every player, with its own leaderboard. Missed a day? Play any of the last 30 from the calendar, and share your result with friends. Every week, the Grand Flow brings one big garden with a leaderboard of its own.

Wander without end

Once the third world is cleared, Wander opens: an endless path of gardens, the same for every player, that slowly get harder. It never costs a life, and a leaderboard shows how far everyone has wandered.

At your own pace

No level timers. Undo, hints and rewinding to the last solvable moment are always free.

Fair lives

A life is used only when you give up a jammed attempt at a new level. Replays, Challenge runs and the Daily Flow never cost one.

Things to collect

Earn up to three stars per garden, reveal Pixel Postcards piece by piece, claim a daily gift, finish daily missions, collect rewards for streaks and gardener levels, and unlock new garden looks. A monthly stamp card, a weekly journey and a gift when you come back.

Yours on every device

Sign in with Apple to keep your garden in iCloud and continue on any iPhone or iPad. Game Center leaderboards and achievements are there if you want them. Widgets, Shortcuts and a Live Activity keep your garden a glance away, and on iPad it plays in landscape too.

Free to play

Pixel Home is free to play. Optional in-app purchases offer extra lives, 24 hours of unlimited lives, Stretch boosters, the Garden Pass (unlimited lives and no ads, for good) and a Supporter Pack, a small thank-you with pieces for your garden.

We collect no data that identifies you, and the ads are never personalized. Read the privacy policy, or visit support if you need a hand.
